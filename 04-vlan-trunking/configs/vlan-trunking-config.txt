LAB 04 — VLAN TRUNKING AND 802.1Q
=================================

Purpose
-------
Configuration record for the 802.1Q trunk between SW1 and SW2.

Topology
--------
SW1 Fa0/24 <-------- 802.1Q Trunk --------> Fa0/24 SW2

VLANs carried across the trunk:
- VLAN 10 — USERS
- VLAN 20 — FINANCE


==================================================
SW1 CONFIGURATION
==================================================

! Enter privileged EXEC mode
enable

! Enter global configuration mode
configure terminal

! Configure the inter-switch link as a trunk
interface FastEthernet0/24
 description TRUNK_TO_SW2
 switchport mode trunk

! Restrict the trunk to the VLANs required by the design
 switchport trunk allowed vlan 10,20

exit

! Return to privileged EXEC mode
end


==================================================
SW2 CONFIGURATION
==================================================

! Enter privileged EXEC mode
enable

! Enter global configuration mode
configure terminal

! Configure the inter-switch link as a trunk
interface FastEthernet0/24
 description TRUNK_TO_SW1
 switchport mode trunk

! Restrict the trunk to the VLANs required by the design
 switchport trunk allowed vlan 10,20

exit

! Return to privileged EXEC mode
end


==================================================
VERIFICATION COMMANDS
==================================================

Run on BOTH SW1 and SW2:

show interfaces trunk

show interfaces fastEthernet 0/24 switchport

show vlan brief

show mac address-table dynamic

Trunking VLANs Enabled: 10,20


==================================================
MAC LEARNING OBSERVATION
==================================================

After traffic was generated across the trunk, MAC addresses from
remote VLAN endpoints were learned through Fa0/24.

SW1 learned remote VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 MAC addresses through Fa0/24.

SW2 learned remote VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 MAC addresses through Fa0/24.

This demonstrates that the trunk is carrying Layer 2 traffic for
both VLAN 10 and VLAN 20.


==================================================
IMPORTANT DESIGN NOTES
==================================================

1. Fa0/24 is the inter-switch link.

2. Fa0/24 is configured as a trunk on BOTH switches.

3. 802.1Q is used for VLAN tagging across the trunk.

4. VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 are explicitly allowed on the trunk.

5. VLAN 1 remains the native VLAN.

6. The trunk does NOT provide inter-VLAN routing.

7. VLAN 10 traffic remains within VLAN 10.

8. VLAN 20 traffic remains within VLAN 20.

9. Devices in VLAN 10 can communicate across the trunk with
   other VLAN 10 devices.

10. Devices in VLAN 20 can communicate across the trunk with
    other VLAN 20 devices.

11. VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 remain separate Layer 2 broadcast domains.

12. Inter-VLAN communication requires a Layer 3 device such as
    a router or multilayer switch.


==================================================
LAB 04 RESULT
==================================================

The inter-switch link between SW1 and SW2 was successfully
configured as an IEEE 802.1Q trunk.

VLAN 10 and VLAN 20 were successfully permitted across the trunk.

Same-VLAN communication across the two switches was verified.

Inter-VLAN communication remained isolated.

MAC address learning demonstrated that remote VLAN endpoints
were reachable through the trunk interface Fa0/24.
